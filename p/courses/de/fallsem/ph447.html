PH 447: Optics and Spectroscopy
Prof. Dinesh Kabra and Prof. Suddhasatta Mahapatra

Prerequisite

A bit of atomic physics and methods in analytical techniques

Course Content

Spatial filtering and spatial coherence, Spin-orbit coupling (QM1 stuff), rotation and absorption spectrum (taught in atomic physics )

Books

Not required, lab manual suffices

Lectures

Not required

Assignments, Exams and Grading

In the online mode, quizzes were conducted every week with questions purely based on lab manual, endsem involved viva in addition to written examination

Tips
